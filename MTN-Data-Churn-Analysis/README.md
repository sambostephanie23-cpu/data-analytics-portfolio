# MTN Customer Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer data for MTN to uncover insights into customer revenue, satisfaction, churn, subscription plans, and device usage.

The analysis was carried out using **Microsoft Excel**, with the final insights presented through an interactive customer analytics dashboard.

The goal of the project is to understand customer behaviour, identify churn patterns, and highlight revenue-generating customer segments that could support better customer retention and business decision-making.

---

## Business Questions

The analysis was designed to answer questions such as:

- How much revenue is generated across different Nigerian states?
- Which states have the highest customer churn rates?
- Which subscription plans generate the most revenue?
- What proportion of customers are active versus churned?
- Which customers generate the highest revenue?
- Which devices are most commonly used by customers?
- How satisfied are customers overall?
- Where should MTN focus its customer retention efforts?

---

## Dataset

The dataset contains **975 customer records** and **18 variables** covering customer demographics, purchasing behaviour, subscription information, satisfaction, revenue, and churn.

### Key Variables

| Category | Variables |
|---|---|
| Customer Information | Customer ID, Full Name, Age, Gender, State |
| Purchase Information | Date of Purchase, Unit Price, Number of Times Purchased, Total Revenue |
| Subscription | Subscription Plan, Customer Tenure |
| Customer Experience | Satisfaction Rate, Customer Review, Customer Response |
| Device Usage | MTN Device, Data Usage |
| Churn | Customer Status, Reasons for Churn |

## Analysis Performed

The analysis focused on several areas of customer performance:

### 1. Customer Overview
Calculated key customer metrics including:

- Total customers
- Average customer satisfaction
- Total revenue
- Customer churn rate

### 2. Revenue Analysis

Revenue was analyzed by:

- State
- Subscription plan
- Individual customer

This helped identify the locations, plans, and customers contributing the most revenue.

### 3. Churn Analysis

Customer churn was analyzed to identify:

- Overall churn rate
- Churn by state
- Active vs. churned customers
- Locations with particularly high churn

### 4. Customer Satisfaction

Customer satisfaction was examined using satisfaction ratings and customer reviews to understand the overall customer experience.

### 5. Device Analysis

Customer device usage was analyzed to identify the most commonly used MTN devices.

## Key Insights

Some of the major findings from the analysis include:

- The dataset contains **975 customers**.
- Total customer revenue is approximately **₦199 million**.
- Average customer satisfaction is approximately **2.95/5**.
- The overall customer churn rate is approximately **29.13%**.
- **Plateau** generated the highest revenue among the states displayed.
- The **1.5TB Yearly Broadband Plan** generated the highest revenue among the subscription plans shown.
- **Abuja (FCT)** recorded the highest churn rate among the states highlighted on the dashboard.
- The **Mobile SIM Card** was the most common device in the dataset.
- A relatively high churn rate indicates an opportunity for MTN to investigate customer dissatisfaction and improve retention strategies.

---

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, MTN could:

1. **Investigate high-churn locations**  
   Focus retention campaigns and customer experience improvements on states with unusually high churn.

2. **Investigate customer dissatisfaction**  
   Analyze the reasons behind low satisfaction scores and identify recurring customer complaints.

3. **Strengthen retention strategies**  
   Introduce targeted offers, loyalty incentives, or personalized plans for customers at risk of churning.

4. **Promote high-performing subscription plans**  
   Identify why certain plans generate significantly more revenue and use those insights to optimize product offerings.

5. **Segment customers by value**  
   High-revenue customers could receive targeted retention and loyalty strategies due to their greater contribution to revenue.
