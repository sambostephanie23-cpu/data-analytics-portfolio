# Nigerian Gas Flare Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes gas flaring activity in Nigeria using environmental and energy-sector data.

The analysis focuses on identifying gas flaring trends, major contributing operators and fields, geographic patterns, estimated carbon emissions, and potential regulatory implications.

## Objectives

- Analyze gas flaring activity across Nigeria.
- Identify operators and fields with significant flaring activity.
- Examine geographic patterns in gas flaring.
- Estimate carbon emissions associated with flaring.
- Estimate potential regulatory fines based on reported flaring volumes.
- Develop insights that can support environmental and operational decision-making.

## Key Questions

- Which operators contribute the most to gas flaring?
- Which oil and gas fields have the highest flaring volumes?
- Where is gas flaring most concentrated?
- How has gas flaring changed over time?
- What are the estimated carbon emissions associated with gas flaring?
- Which operators or fields may require greater environmental attention?
